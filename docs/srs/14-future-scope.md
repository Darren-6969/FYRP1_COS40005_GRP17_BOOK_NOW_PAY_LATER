# 14. Future Scope

1. Waitlist: customers queue automatically when inventory is full and are promoted in order when a booking is cancelled
2. Vehicle handover management: condition inspection at pickup and return, fuel level, photographs and damage recording, completing the rental operational loop
3. Corporate and group accounts: business registration, consolidated billing and multi-person booking management
4. Chatbot depth: image recognition, voice interaction and multiple languages
5. Multi-region and multi-currency: expansion across Southeast Asia
6. Operator intelligence: SARIMA-driven inventory optimisation, dynamic pricing and promotion timing recommendations
7. Credit tier benefits: positive incentives for Trusted customers such as reduced deposits on premium listings, extending the credit system from penalty to reward
